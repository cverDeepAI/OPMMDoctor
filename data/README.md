# Data / 数据说明

The OPMM Dataset comprises whole-body multi-joint radiographs and structured clinical
indicators from three collaborating hospitals, covering 9 anatomical sites.

**Patient privacy / 隐私**：受试者影像与临床数据涉及隐私与伦理限制，不在本仓库公开。
去标识化数据可在合理请求下、经相应伦理与数据使用协议后向通讯作者申请获取。

Data splitting is performed at the **patient level** (all images/ROIs/clinical records of a
patient stay within one fold) to avoid patient-level leakage.
